# KPMG_IS

## Project Track
**Track D: Accounting Information Systems (AIS)**

## WOC Problem Statement
**How should Wasatch Outfitters Collective (WOC) modernize its expense management process using an Accounting Information System that improves efficiency, financial controls, and reporting?**

## Project Overview
We are serving as an Information Systems consulting team engaged by WOC. Our work evaluates the current expense reimbursement process, identifies workflow inefficiencies and control weaknesses, and recommends a practical AIS solution. The goal is to automate approvals and reimbursements, improve auditability, and provide better information for financial decision making.

Our focus is on information systems and business process improvement—not conducting an accounting audit or preparing financial statements.

## Consulting Focus
- Accounting Information Systems and financial data management
- Expense workflow automation and approval routing
- Internal controls, audit trails, and compliance
- Business process improvement and management reporting

## Business Objectives
The recommended solution should help WOC:
- Reduce unmatched expenses and missing receipts.
- Automate expense approvals and reimbursement workflows.
- Strengthen financial controls and audit trails.
- Improve visibility into departmental spending and budgets.
- Increase the efficiency and accuracy of month-end reconciliation.

## Key Stakeholder
**Accounting staff** need an automated workflow that reduces manual reconciliation, improves accuracy, and minimizes time spent tracking missing documentation.
